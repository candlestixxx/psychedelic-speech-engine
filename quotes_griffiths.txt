Psilocybin can occasion mystical type experiences having substantial and sustained personal meaning and spiritual significance.
Under carefully controlled conditions, psilocybin can produce experiences similar to spontaneously occurring mystical experiences.
The experience of unity is at the heart of the mystical experience.
These substances have the potential to profoundly transform how we understand consciousness.
A single high dose session can produce enduring changes in personality.
Openness and wellbeing can increase months after a single psychedelic experience.
