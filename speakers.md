## Foundational Pioneers
- **Albert Hofmann** (1906–2008) — Swiss chemist; synthesized LSD-25 (1938), discovered its effects (1943); isolated psilocybin/psilocin.
- **Alexander "Sasha" Shulgin** (1925–2014) — synthesized/bioassayed hundreds of compounds; rediscovered & popularized MDMA; PiHKAL/TiHKAL.
- **Ann Shulgin** (1931–2022) — co-author of PiHKAL/TiHKAL.
- **Maria Sabina** (1894–1985) — Mazatec curandera; sacred psilocybin veladas; bridge to Gordon Wasson (1955).
- **Aldous Huxley** (1894–1963) — The Doors of Perception (1954); mescaline as a perceptual/philosophical lens.

## Counterculture & Early Clinical Era
- **Timothy Leary** (1920–1996) — Harvard Psilocybin Project; "Turn on, tune in, drop out."
- **Ram Dass (Richard Alpert)** (1931–2019) — Leary's Harvard partner; Be Here Now.
- **Stanislav Grof** (b. 1931) — LSD-assisted psychotherapy; Holotropic Breathwork.
- **Terence McKenna** (1946–2000) — ethnobotanist; plant hallucinogens, shamanism, "Stoned Ape" hypothesis.

## Contemporary Leaders & Scientists
- **Rick Doblin** (b. 1953) — founder, MAPS; decades of MDMA/psilocybin-assisted therapy regulatory work.
- **Roland Griffiths** (1946–2023) — Johns Hopkins; landmark 2006 psilocybin study; modern psychedelic renaissance.
- **Robin Carhart-Harris** — UCSF Psychedelics Division (ex-Imperial College); neuroimaging; "Entropic Brain" theory.
