The psychedelic state is characterized by a more entropic, more disordered brain state.
Psychedelics may work by relaxing the brain's high level constraints.
The brain on psychedelics enters a more flexible and fluid mode of functioning.
This relaxed state allows normally segregated brain networks to communicate more freely.
The entropic brain hypothesis offers a new framework for understanding mental health.
Depression and addiction may reflect overly rigid patterns of brain activity.
